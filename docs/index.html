Frequently Asked Questions

How does a check-in work?

Take four full-body photos — front, back, left side, and right side — and submit them. Morph's AI analyzes your physique and returns scores, estimated body composition, personalized macro targets, and a training plan.

What's the difference between Free and Pro?

Free members get one AI check-in per week. Pro members can check in every day for faster feedback and more detailed progress tracking.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Morph, then tap Cancel. You'll keep Pro access until the end of your current billing period.

How do I delete my account and data?

Open the app, go to the Profile tab, scroll to Account, and tap Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, photos, and all check-in history from your device.

Where are my photos stored?

Your photos and check-in history are stored locally on your device. When you request an analysis, your photos are securely transmitted to our AI provider solely to generate that analysis. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I get the best results?

Use consistent lighting, the same time of day, the same relaxed poses, and fitted clothing every week. Progress photos work by comparison — keep every variable the same except your body. The app includes a full photo guide on the check-in screen.

Is Morph a substitute for professional advice?

No. Morph provides fitness and nutrition guidance for informational purposes only. AI-generated scores and estimates are approximations, not clinical measurements. Consult a qualified professional before making significant changes to your diet or exercise routine.
