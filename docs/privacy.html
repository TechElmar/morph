Privacy Policy

Morph — AI Physique Coach · Last updated: August 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Morph ("we," "our," or "the app") handles your information. By using Morph, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information You Provide

To use Morph, you create an account and a profile. This includes:

  • Account details: your name, email address, and a password (stored only as a securely hashed value).
  • Profile data: age, gender, height, weight, goal weight, fitness goal, and activity level.
  • Physique photos: the four progress photos you submit for each check-in.
  • Optional profile picture.

2. How Your Data Is Stored

Your account, profile, check-in history, and photos are stored securely in the cloud using Supabase, our hosted backend provider, so that your data is available when you sign in on any device. Passwords are handled by Supabase's authentication service and are never stored by us in readable form. Your data is protected by row-level security, meaning each account can access only its own information. Photos are kept in a private storage bucket that only your authenticated account can read.

3. AI Analysis and Photo Processing

When you request a physique analysis, your four photos and relevant profile details (such as height, weight, age, and goal) are securely transmitted to our AI provider, Anthropic (the Claude API), for the sole purpose of generating your analysis. Requests are routed through a secure server-side proxy so that our API credentials are never exposed in the app.

Anthropic processes this data to return your results and, per Anthropic's policies, does not use API inputs to train its models. Your photos are transmitted only when you actively submit a check-in. For more information, see Anthropic's privacy practices at anthropic.com/legal/privacy.

4. Subscriptions and Payments

Morph offers an optional auto-renewing subscription (Morph Pro). All purchases and payment processing are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive or store your payment card details.

5. Notifications

If you enable reminders, Morph schedules local notifications on your device (for example, check-in reminders and daily motivation). These are generated on-device and are not sent from or through our servers.

6. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or analytics tracking SDKs.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

7. Deleting Your Data

You are always in control of your data. Open the app, go to the Profile tab, and tap Delete Account to permanently remove your profile, photos, and all check-in history from our backend. If you need any assistance deleting your data, contact us at the email below and we will remove it.

8. Children's Privacy

Morph is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy or your data, contact us at morph.ai.support@gmail.com.
